05 · Motion

Springs, never timers

Four rune-motion springs. Retargets keep their velocity, so a panel closed mid-swell turns around instead of restarting. Small things move fast, drops move with weight, and nothing ever holds perfectly still.

Lead · the thing you look at moves first, then its container. A row's text leads its background by 0.2.
Stagger · 28 ms between siblings, at most 8 of them, so a full panel lands in under 420 ms.
Reduced motion · no travel, no necks. Opacity and colour take 150 ms, shaders hold their first frame.

09 · Handoff

For the build

TokensEvery --yz-* name maps one to one to yozora_ui::tok. Colours in OKLCH as Rune stores them; springs as Spring::new(duration, bounce).
SurfacesBar: layer Top, exclusive zone 44. Drops: one overlay layer per output that draws its own neck over the bar edge. Lock: ext-session-lock. Rune has layer-shell overlays but neither of these yet, so they go in a fork patch.
BudgetOne draw call per frame, shaders culled at intensity 0. Wallpaper fx ticks at 30 Hz, UI at the output rate: 144 and 75 here. GPU released when a drop is closed.
10 · Styles

Four worlds, one desktop

A style is not a theme. It replaces everything you see and how it moves, and nothing you use. Switch in Settings, Appearance; the change lands in one curtain.

A style ownsTokens, type, shape, the bar's form, the signature entrance and exit, springs, effects, wallpaper, the lock and its unlock. Each ships its own accent; there is no accent picker.
Never changesWhere everything is: bar slots, popover anchors, the glyph set, the information in every panel, keyboard paths and labels. Muscle memory survives a style change.
For agents and scriptsyozora style set folio, yozora style get, yozora fx set calm, yozora style set tension --follow-sun. Effects Full, Calm and Still apply to every style; reduced motion forces Still.

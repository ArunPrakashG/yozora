Rules

  1. Only transform and opacity move. Shadow, blur, size and large color fills never animate.
  2. At rest, nothing draws. The frame clock runs only while a spring is moving.
  3. Interrupt, don't restart. A new target keeps the spring's velocity.
  4. Timers use the wall clock. A dropped frame never stretches a toast.
  5. Leaving is faster than arriving. Exits are 140 ms and never wait for anything.
  6. Reduced motion is a 100 ms fade. No travel, no scale.
